🇫🇷 Paris Itinerary

June 1 - June 5 (4 nights)

Overview

Dates: June 1 - June 5 (4 nights)

Flight: Air France from NYC

Base: AC Hotel Paris Porte Maillot

Monday (June 1): Arrival + Eiffel Area Icons

Theme: Easy arrival, golden-hour photos, no rushing

1:00 PM — Check in to hotel

  • AC Hotel Paris Porte Maillot

3:30 PM — Lunch @ Le Petit Maillot

5:00 PM — Port Debilly

  • One of the BEST Eiffel + Seine photo spots in Paris
  • Best light + fewer crowds than Trocadéro

7:00 PM — Walk under the Eiffel Tower

  • Slow stroll, soak it in

8:30 PM — Eiffel Tower sparkle show (first sparkle after sunset)

  • Optional drink nearby

💡 Why this works: Zero stress after travel, iconic photos immediately.

Tuesday (June 2): Versailles Day Trip

Theme: Grand & freeing

Morning → Afternoon

12:00 PM — Palace of Versailles

  • Bike rental + gardens = elite choice
  • Palace interior + fountains + countryside feel

Evening — Return to Paris

  • Casual dinner near hotel or Saint-Germain
  • Nolinski Cocktail Bar

💡 No changes needed here — this day is perfect.

Wednesday (June 3): Saint-Germain → Opera → Luxe Paris

Theme: Chic Paris + luxury visuals

8:00 AM — Café de Flore

  • Coffee + croissant + classic Paris café shots

9:00 AM — Walk Saint-Germain streets (shopping & vibe)

10:00–11:15 AM — La Galerie Dior

12:00 PM — Palais Garnier

  • Interior is insane for photos (staircase + ceiling)

1:30 PM — Galeries Lafayette Haussmann

  • Glass dome photos
  • Optional rooftop Eiffel view (FREE)

3:00 PM - 7:00 PM — Louvre Museum

Evening — Dinner near area + Night time Seine Cruise to see the Eiffel Tower

Thursday (June 4): Montmartre Magic + Eiffel Finale

Theme: Bohemian charm + romantic finish

8:00 AM — Montmartre

Stops in order (all walkable):

  • Wall of Love
  • Place du Tertre
  • La Maison Rose
  • La Bohème
  • Basilique du Sacré-Cœur

📸 This area alone can fill your camera roll.

Afternoon — Louis Vuitton & Champs-Élysées + Arc De Triomphe

5:30 PM — Eiffel Tower lawn picnic

  • Wine + baguette + relaxed golden hour

8:00 PM — Rooftop dinner @ Girafe (as planned) + Nightclub @ Pachamama Paris (maybe not)

Friday (June 5): Champs-Élysées Send-Off

Theme: Power Paris goodbye

6:00 AM — Airport transfer

9:00 AM — Flight to Barcelona ✈️
