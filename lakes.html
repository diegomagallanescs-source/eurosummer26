🇮🇹 Lake Lugano / Lake Como Itinerary

June 18 - June 21 (3 nights)

Overview

Dates: June 18 - June 21 (3 nights)

Arrival: Train from Milan

Base: Parco San Marco Lifestyle Beach Resort

Thursday (June 18): Arrival & Lake Lugano Boat

1:00 PM — Arrive in Lake Lugano, Check in to Hotel

2:00 PM — Lunch at Hotel

4:00 PM — 1hr Private Boat on Lake Lugano

  • Scenic cruise along the Lugano coastline
  • Views of Monte Brè and Monte San Salvatore

5:30 PM — Return to Hotel

7:30 PM — Sunset & Relax at Resort

Friday (June 19): Villa del Balbianello Day

9:00 AM — Breakfast at Hotel

10:00 AM — Private Transfer to Lenno (Lake Como)

11:00 AM — Visit Villa del Balbianello

  • Explore famous gardens and terraces
  • See the iconic Lake Como gates viewpoint
  • Photography around the historic villa grounds

1:30 PM — Lunch in Lenno (Lakeside)

3:30 PM — Walk around Lenno harbor & enjoy lake views

5:00 PM — Private Transfer back to Hotel

Saturday (June 20): Lake Como Day

8:00 AM — Private transfer to Menaggio from hotel

9:00 AM — Ferry to Bellagio

11:00 AM — Explore Bellagio old town

  • Gardens, shopping, photos

1:00 PM — Lunch lakeside in Bellagio @ La Punta

2:00 PM — Ferry to Varenna

3:00 PM — Walk Lovers Path with Gelato + Lake Views

4:30 PM — 1hr scenic boat tour (Lake Como)

6:00 PM — Ferry Back to Menaggio and Private Transfer back to Hotel

Sunday (June 21): Train to Rome 🚆

11:00 AM — Go to train station

12:00 PM — Take train to Milan / Rome
